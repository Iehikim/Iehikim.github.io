Research

Research Areas

We develop mathematical models and optimization algorithms for the design and operation of manufacturing, logistics and service systems.

Introduction →

01Planning

Demand forecasting, inventory control, production planning, process planning and capacity planning.

02Scheduling

Operations, service and project scheduling, and routing & scheduling problems (TSP, VRP, OP, TOP).

03Design

Supply chain and network design, facility layout and configuration selection of manufacturing systems.

04Environmentally Conscious Design & Manufacturing

Reverse logistics, remanufacturing systems, disassembly planning and disassembly lot-sizing.

05Theoretical Backgrounds

Operations research and operations management, linear and integer programming, and probability & statistics.

06Methodological Backgrounds

Exact (optimal) and heuristic approaches, reinforcement learning, deep learning and simulation.
