김현일 (Hyeon-Il Kim), Ph.D.

Postdoctoral Researcher at KTH Royal Institute of Technology, Stockholm

I am a Postdoctoral Researcher in the Department of Engineering Mechanics and the Centre for ECO2 Vehicle Design at KTH Royal Institute of Technology. My research covers operations scheduling, production planning, combinatorial optimization, the design and operation of manufacturing systems, environmentally conscious design and manufacturing, sustainable and circular closed-loop supply chains, and applications of operations research.

I welcome opportunities to collaborate with researchers and industry practitioners on challenging real-world problems. Please get in touch if you are interested in working together.
